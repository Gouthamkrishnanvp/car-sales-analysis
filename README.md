# Car Sales Dashboard

### Project Overview
Developed an interactive Car Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, pricing trends, and dealer-wise distribution. The dashboard tracks $371M+ YTD revenue, 13K cars sold, and average price trends, enabling data-driven insights through KPI cards, weekly sales trends, and regional performance maps. Implemented filters for body style, dealer, transmission, and engine type to support dynamic analysis and improve business decision-making.

### Exploratory Data analysis 

EDA involved exploring the sales data to answer key questions, such as :

1. Which car body style contributes the highest share of total sales revenue?
2. How do weekly sales trends change throughout the year and when are the peak sales periods?
3. Which dealer regions and car companies sell the highest number of vehicles and generate the most revenue?

### Result/Findings

1. Total car sales reached $371M+ YTD with a 24% growth, showing a strong increase in overall sales performance.
2. Cars sold increased by 25% (13K units), indicating higher market demand and improved dealership performance.
3. SUV and Hatchback body styles contributed the largest share of revenue, making them the most popular vehicle categories among customers.
